The Phantom Revenue

Problem
Product reported GBP 737,014.26 for August revenue; Finance reported GBP 682,680.51. Management needed one defensible figure.
Finding
The teams treated cancellations differently. 278 cancellation invoices (GBP −54,333.75) explained the entire gap, leaving GBP 0.00 unexplained.
Recommendation
Report Net Invoice Revenue, label the gross figure separately, and run a gross + cancellations = net check on every refresh.

SQL · SQLite · Cohort analysis

The Retention Mirage

Problem
A CRM dashboard showed repeat purchase rate falling from 72.55% to 28.80%, and Marketing requested GBP 30,000 for a voucher campaign.
Finding
Older cohorts had months to repeat; newer ones had weeks. On a fixed 30-day window, the mature July–October cohorts showed no continuous decline.
Recommendation
Do not approve the campaign as proposed. Test it with a randomized pilot measuring incremental repeat purchases and contribution margin.
